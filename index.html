FAQ

Good to know.

Is the measuring visit really free?

Yes. We come to you, measure every opening and talk through the options. There is no obligation to order.

How soon will I get an offer?

You get a written offer within 48 hours of the measuring visit.

PVC or aluminium — which should I choose?

PVC insulates well and usually costs less. Aluminium allows slimmer frames and larger panes, which is why it's often chosen for sliding systems and big glazing. We'll help you decide at the visit.

Do you remove the old windows?

Yes — removing the old frames can be included in your offer, and our team does it as part of the installation.

Is the online estimate the final price?

No. It's a rough demo range to give you an idea. The final price comes in the written offer after the free measuring visit.
